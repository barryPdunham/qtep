# QTEP Disclosure Templates

Use the disclosure that most accurately reflects the circumstances of an
evaluation. Modify it when necessary rather than using language that is
technically inaccurate.

## Independent Evaluation

> This is an independent, uncompensated evaluation. The evaluator has no
> material financial or professional relationship with the evaluated project
> or its maintainers. The evaluation assesses developer functionality and
> utility and does not constitute a security audit or assurance of production
> readiness.

## Prior Experience

> This is an uncompensated evaluation. The evaluator has previously used the
> evaluated software, as disclosed in the evaluation plan. Prior experience
> was not treated as evaluation evidence; conclusions are based on the tests
> conducted under the identified QTEP version.

## Competitive Interest

> The evaluator or evaluator's organization has a material interest in
> technology that may overlap or compete with the evaluated project. This
> relationship is disclosed because it may create a perceived or actual
> conflict of interest. The evaluation methodology, evidence, and assumptions
> are provided so that readers can independently assess the conclusion.

## Internal Evaluation

> This software is developed by or affiliated with the evaluator or evaluator's
> organization. QTEP is being used as an internal development and quality
> methodology. This is not an independent QTEP review.

## Additional Disclosure

Document any other relationship or circumstance that a reasonable reader
would consider relevant when assessing the independence of the evaluation.
