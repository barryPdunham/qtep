## 10. Evaluation Budget and Stop Conditions

### Target Effort

[Estimate]

### Stop Conditions

Testing stops when:

- the defined claim has sufficient evidence;
- the equivalence gate fails;
- the relevant uncertainty has been resolved;
- further work would constitute debugging or development rather than evaluation;
- additional testing would exceed reasonable scope without materially
  strengthening the conclusion.
